# United Rentals (united-rentals)

United Rentals is the world's largest equipment rental company, headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut. The company provides a broad selection of APIs to help customers simplify the procure-to-pay lifecycle for equipment rentals. United Rentals offers system integration through its Total Control platform, supporting EDI, cXML, JSON, and flat-file formats to connect with customer procurement and ERP systems.

## APIs

### United Rentals Total Control Integration API

United Rentals provides a broad selection of APIs to simplify the procure-to-pay lifecycle for equipment rentals. The Total Control platform supports system integration via EDI, cXML, JSON, spreadsheet, and flat-file formats. Customers can integrate with their procurement or ERP systems for punch-out catalog ordering, automated invoicing, PO management, fleet management, and bill pay. Processes hundreds of secure customer integrations.

- **Human URL:** [https://www.unitedrentals.com/services/online-services/total-control/system-integration](https://www.unitedrentals.com/services/online-services/total-control/system-integration)
- **Base URL:** `https://api.unitedrentals.com/v1`
